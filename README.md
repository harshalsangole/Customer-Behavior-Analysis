# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using transactional data from 3,900 purchases across various product categories[cite: 2]. The goal is to uncover insights into spending patterns, customer segments, product preferences, and subscription behavior to guide strategic business decisions and optimize marketing strategies[cite: 1, 2].

## Tech Stack
* **Python (Pandas, SQLAlchemy, psycopg2):** Data cleaning, missing value imputation, and database integration[cite: 2, 12].
* **PostgreSQL:** Structured data analysis and business transaction querying[cite: 3].
* **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development for visual data storytelling[cite: 1, 8].

## Key Insights
* **Demographics & Spending:** Male customers generated significantly higher revenue ($157,890) compared to female customers ($75,191)[cite: 4]. The "Young Adult" demographic is the top-earning age group, contributing $62,143 in total revenue[cite: 8].
* **Subscriptions:** 73% of the customer base are non-subscribers, while 27% are subscribed[cite: 8]. Customers with over 5 previous purchases show a strong potential for conversion, though the majority currently remain non-subscribers[cite: 7].
* **Product Performance:** Clothing is the highest-performing category in both total sales and revenue, followed by Accessories[cite: 8]. The most frequently purchased clothing items are blouses, pants, and shirts[cite: 7]. 
* **Discounts:** Products like Hats, Sneakers, and Coats see the highest dependency on discounts, with roughly 47% to 50% of their sales involving a promotional offer[cite: 6].

## Business Recommendations
1. **Boost Subscriptions:** Promote exclusive benefits for subscribers to increase the current 27% adoption rate[cite: 8, 9].
2. **Customer Loyalty Programs:** Reward repeat buyers to transition them into the highly valuable "Loyal" segment[cite: 9].
3. **Review Discount Policy:** Balance sales boosts with margin control[cite: 9].
4. **Product Positioning:** Highlight top-rated and best-selling products in campaigns[cite: 9].
5. **Targeted Marketing:** Focus campaign efforts and promotional spending on high-revenue age groups (Young Adults) and customers utilizing express shipping[cite: 9].

4. **Report and Presentation:** Summary of key findings and recommendations[cite: 1].
